# Field notes

Practical things that are not in the law: what usually happens, what people
choose and why. The article writer reads them before drafting, and the fact
checker checks an article against them. The rules for using this file are in
[articles.md](../product/articles.md).

Each note says where it comes from: Azamat, a message in a chat (a `t.me` link
to the message, never a person's name) or an official source. Experience is
written as experience («часто», «многие выбирают»), never as a rule; an
article that uses a note says so in the same words. A number in a note is
checked against its official source before an article states it.

Every review Azamat gives on an article becomes a note here or a row in the
[glossary](glossary.md), not only a fix in that article.

## admission

### Nostrification through a krajský úřad often means exams

- **Note:** nostrifikace through a krajský úřad often needs exams, and
  preparing for them takes a lot of time. Advice that nostrifikace «is more
  convenient when applying to several universities» misleads without this.
- **Source:** Azamat, review of the nostrification article (#503).
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

### A faculty's assessment is not nostrification

- **Note:** some faculties assess the certificate themselves for admission.
  That holds only for admission to that faculty and does not replace
  nostrifikace (for work, another university, other documents). An article
  never presents it as a replacement, does not put it in «Коротко», and names
  no faculties or fees for it: the reader is sent to the faculty's admission
  page.
- **Source:** Azamat, review of the nostrification article (#503); it replaces
  Azamat's earlier remark the same day that many pay each faculty about
  1 000 Kč instead.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

### Students call the faculty route «внутренняя нострификация»

- **Note:** in chats the faculty's own assessment is called «внутренняя
  нострификация» and is often recommended over the krajský úřad route. An
  article still does not present it as a replacement for nostrifikace: it
  counts only for that faculty's admission (see «A faculty's assessment is not
  nostrification» above). At ČVUT students say the faculty lists the documents
  on its site and decides within about a month, without an exam; one student sent a certified
  translation of the certificate and its supplement with grades and hours by
  post. Some went to the faculty in person and asked for a faster review when
  the deadline was close.
- **Source:** chats: https://t.me/cvut_fa/8088, https://t.me/cvut_chat/167056,
  https://t.me/cvut_fel/42299.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the document list and the time at each faculty an article names.

### ČVUT checks a foreign certificate centrally

- **Note:** at ČVUT the rectorate, not each faculty, assesses a foreign
  certificate for admission to any of its faculties (FIT, FEL, FS and the
  others). The fee is 950 Kč, paid once; applications for 2027/28 open on
  1 December 2026. At FF UK the page names 960 Kč, but for 2025/26. Not for
  articles as a replacement for nostrifikace (Azamat's note); use only on a
  faculty's own page or when an article names ČVUT's procedure.
- **Source:** official: https://www.cvut.cz/posouzeni-vzdelani-pro-prijimaci-rizeni-na-cvut
  (read 2026-11-03).
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** FF UK for 2027/28.

### The krajský úřad often sets 3–4 exam subjects

- **Note:** students say the office sends a list of subjects to sit in
  Czechia, usually 3–4, depending on the subjects with fewer hours than the
  Czech programme. The law allows 1 to 5.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/cvut_fa/8082; the 1–5 range is on
  portal.gov.cz (nostrifikace zahraničního vysvědčení).
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

### Without the document there is no enrolment

- **Note:** applicants allowed to bring the certificate later still need the
  nostrifikace or the faculty's assessment uploaded before enrolment; at
  VŠCHT students mention a July deadline to attach it in SIS.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/vscht_chat/63844, https://t.me/vscht_chat/63841.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** each faculty's current deadline.

### MUNI e-přihláška: which «previous education» to choose

- **Note:** students advise foreigners to choose «ze střední školy», not «Dům
  zahraničních služeb MŠMT», unless they really file through that institution.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/masaryk_chat/40840.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the current MUNI e-přihláška form.

### Schools are sometimes asked for more than the law lists

- **Note:** students mention being asked for the school's licence and a
  confirmation that the certificate gives the right to enter university. One
  student calls such extra demands unlawful and appealable. Write it as
  experience; the documents the law asks for are in zákon 561/2004 Sb. § 108
  odst. 3–4.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/masaryk_chat/40152, https://t.me/cvut_fit/241176.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

## housing

### Book a dormitory early

- **Note:** places that are easy to get go in the first weeks after booking
  opens; students who booked months later found little. When a semester
  starts, places free up from those who did not come.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/karlov_chat/104333, https://t.me/karlov_chat/105150.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

### VUT dormitories: ask for free places in all blocks

- **Note:** students advise writing to the dormitory office, in Czech, asking
  about free places in all blocks, and then asking for a reservation.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/vut_chat/66031, https://t.me/vut_chat/66081.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

### MUNI dormitories: what cooking devices are allowed

- **Note:** students say only air fryers are allowed, they must be
  registered, and an unregistered device can bring a fine (a figure of
  5 000 Kč is mentioned).
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/masaryk_chat/40850.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the SKM MU accommodation rules before an article states it.

## study

### The first year has a minimum of credits

- **Note:** students say a newcomer must earn at least 15 credits in the first
  two semesters, or the study ends; for a student with a study residence
  permit that also puts the permit at risk. Extra easy courses are a common
  way to reach the minimum.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/cvut_fsv/97835, https://t.me/vscht_chat/64164.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the studijní a zkušební řád of each university.

### A registered optional course can become compulsory

- **Note:** at VUT FEKT students report that a povinně volitelný course, once
  registered, must be passed.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/vut_chat/65896.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the current FEKT rules.

### If a course is full, write to the teacher

- **Note:** students who could not register for a course wrote to the
  teacher or the studijní oddělení and were sometimes added.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/karlov_chat/104670, https://t.me/cvut_fit/245710.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

## work

### The «20 hours a week on DPP» belief is common

- **Note:** students repeat that a DPP allows at most 20 hours a week. The
  law sets 300 hours a year per employer for DPP (zákon 262/2006 Sb. § 75);
  the 20 hours belong to DPČ (§ 76). An article on work answers this belief
  directly.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/karlov_chat/104460; the law as cited.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.

## visa

### After 60 days without a decision

- **Note:** students say that from the 61st day after a complete application
  they write a complaint about the delay. Which remedy applies (for example a
  request against inaction under the správní řád) is not yet checked.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/vscht_chat/63915.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the remedy and the office, on mv.gov.cz or ipc.gov.cz.

### Free legal advice for foreigners

- **Note:** students point to the Integrační centrum Praha, which holds
  regular legal consultations for foreigners.
- **Source:** chat: https://t.me/karlov_chat/105339.
- **Date:** 2026-11-03.
- **To check:** the centre's current page and schedule.
